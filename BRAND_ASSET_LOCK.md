## Typography

- Primary headlines: Barlow Condensed ExtraBold Italic, weight 800 italic
- Secondary headings and interface labels: Barlow Condensed Bold, weight 700
- Supporting and body text: Barlow Condensed Medium or Regular, weights 500 and 400

The font files are self-hosted through the `@fontsource/barlow-condensed` package.
